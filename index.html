Save Instagram reels in seconds.

Paste a public reel, video or photo link. Download it to your phone or computer in the original quality. No app, no login.

How to download a reel

  1. Open the reel in Instagram, tap Share, then Copy link.
  2. Paste the link above. InstaSave starts fetching right away.
  3. Pick MP4, JPG or MP3 and tap the download button.

Why InstaSave

Questions

Is InstaSave free?

Yes. There is no subscription and no limit on downloads.

Can I download from private accounts?

No. InstaSave only works with public posts.

Do you store my downloads?

No. Files are fetched when you ask and are not kept on our servers.

Where do the files go?

Into your Downloads folder. On many phones they also appear in Gallery or Photos.

Can I reuse what I download?

Only with the creator’s permission. Downloads are for personal, offline viewing, not commercial use.
